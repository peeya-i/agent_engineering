# WidgetWare Renewal Discount Policy

Policy owner: Revenue Operations  
Version: 2026.2  
Scope: Renewal discounts for annual recurring revenue (ARR)

## Approval bands

Approval is based on the requested percentage discount from list renewal ARR.

| Requested discount | Required approval |
| --- | --- |
| 0%–5% | Account Executive |
| More than 5%–10% | Regional Sales Manager |
| More than 10%–15% | VP Sales and Finance Business Partner |
| More than 15%–20% | Chief Revenue Officer and Finance Director |
| More than 20% | CFO and Chief Revenue Officer; exception memo required |

The upper boundary in each row is inclusive. For example, exactly 10% requires Regional Sales Manager approval; exactly 15% requires VP Sales and Finance Business Partner approval.

## Additional rules

- Never split a discount across line items to avoid an approval band.
- Quote arithmetic must use the deterministic calculator when the user asks for dollar discount or net ARR.
- A discount approval does not approve non-standard legal terms.
- Do not describe a requested discount as approved until all named approvers have approved it.
